Fasomo

A fasting tracker for the Hijri calendar, in English and Arabic. It shows the recommended (Sunnah) and obligatory fasting days, reminds you before each one, and keeps track of what you have fasted and what you still owe.

Features

  • Fasting days: White Days, Monday and Thursday, Arafah, Tasua and Ashura, the Six Days of Shawwal, and Ramadan. Dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar.
  • A Hijri calendar from 1356 to 1500 AH, with each fasting type colour-coded.
  • Ramadan mode: suhoor and iftar times for your city, prayer times and a daily checklist.
  • Reminders for every fasting type: the evening before, the morning of, or a time you choose.
  • A fasting log with streaks and monthly and yearly totals.
  • A make-up (Qada) tracker with its own reminders.
  • Duas and intentions with their sources.
  • Announcements, such as the confirmed start of Ramadan or Eid.
  • Light and dark themes; Arabic with a right-to-left layout.
